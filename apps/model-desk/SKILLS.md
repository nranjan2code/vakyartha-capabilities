# Model Desk: skills

## Ships with this app

None. Model Desk has no chat and makes no model requests.

## Vakyartha skills that apply

- `app-operator`: opening the app, Refresh, receipts and background operation.
- `app-maintainer`: changing the package, its surfaces and these documents, and running the tests.
- `app-debugger`: finding why a refresh was blocked or failed, or a view does not behave as expected.
- `app-admin`: reviewing Access, the Web pages tool and its destination, and Background operation.

## Requires

- integration:web
