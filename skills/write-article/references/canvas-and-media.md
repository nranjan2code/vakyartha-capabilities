# Media and spoken projection

Layout belongs to the host: an article is laid out as an article page, one reading-width column, by the shared presentation procedure. This file covers the content an article supplies around its text.

## Media

1. **Lead image**: when a picture serves the article, give one after the headline and byline, with alternative text that describes it and a caption or credit.
2. **Figures**: place a chart, diagram or figure next to the claim it supports, with alternative text and a caption naming its source and date.
3. **Several images**: group images that are compared or read in sequence, each with its own alternative text.
4. **Audio**: narration, interviews or excerpts are offered with a description of what they hold.
5. **Sources of media**: use secure public addresses (`https://`) or verified local media paths only. Never expose credentials, local file system paths or download scripts.

## Placement and spoken projection

1. Long reads belong on the canvas; the chat reply gives the headline, the thesis and the takeaways only.
2. Spoken output reads the headline, the deck and the takeaways, in the article's language, and leaves out addresses, footnote numbers and table rows.
