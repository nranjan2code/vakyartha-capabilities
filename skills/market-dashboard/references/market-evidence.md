# Market evidence and return basis

Keep exchange, ticker, series, requested time zone, quote units and exact comparable session dates together. A delayed quote describes its observation time, and a saved snapshot does not establish live prices.

A window return uses (last close / first close − 1) × 100 from verified comparable closes. Record both endpoints and their dates. Keep price and dividend-inclusive total return distinct. A spot quote cannot supply historical returns or OHLC records.
